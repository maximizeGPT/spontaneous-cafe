The site has a home page, four service pages (Foraging, Private Chef, Catering & Events, Cooking Classes), an About page and a contact form. ## How to answer

Half an hour, in one sitting or in pieces. Type under each question, or record a voice note and send it. "Yes, that is right" is a complete answer. "I don't know" or "it depends" is also useful, so say that instead of skipping. Anything you do not answer stays as my guess, marked on the page, so nothing goes live wrong by accident. Answers back within a week would let me launch the week after.
