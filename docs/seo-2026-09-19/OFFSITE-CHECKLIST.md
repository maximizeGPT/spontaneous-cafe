## Marketplaces, a business decision

Source: facts verified 2026-09-19 by follow-up research, except where marked unverified. Every item here trades a fee, a commission or a nofollow link for a specific audience. Matt is the one who should decide which trade is worth making.

- [ ] **Mohammed + Matt:** [Airbnb Experiences](https://www.airbnb.com/host/experiences). Free to apply, but Airbnb deducts a 20 percent service fee from every booking and requires liability insurance. Applications have been open since May 2025. Listings carry no website link. This is the strongest audience match on this list: Matt's guests are already San Francisco weekenders in Mendocino rentals, exactly who books an Experience. (estimate: a few hours to apply, then an approval wait)
- [ ] **Mohammed + Matt:** [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/organizer/overview/). Free events cost nothing to list. Paid tickets carry 3.7 percent plus $1.79 a ticket plus 2.9 percent processing, and the fee can be passed to the buyer. The organiser website link is nofollow. Only worth setting up once classes have public dates. (estimate: 30 minutes, once there are dates)
- [ ] **Mohammed + Matt:** [WeddingPro](https://www.weddingpro.com/signup). One free signup puts a storefront on both The Knot and WeddingWire. The website link needs a paid plan, and stays nofollow even then. Paid tier prices are sales-quoted, and the figures circulating for them are unverified. (estimate: 15 minutes for the free signup)
- [ ] **Mohammed + Matt:** [Tripadvisor Things to Do](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Owners). A free operator listing is available without Viator. The listing rule asks for one permanent address, which is the real obstacle for a service-area business with no storefront. Listings carry no website link. (estimate: 30 minutes to attempt)
- [ ] **Mohammed + Matt:** Skip Viator. $29 per experience listing, plus an unpublished commission that runs up to 50 percent under its Accelerate programme. (estimate: none)
- [ ] **Mohammed + Matt:** Skip Thumbtack. Pay per lead, no published prices, no website link at all. (estimate: none)
- [ ] **Mohammed + Matt:** Skip Bark. $2.35 a credit, credits expire in three months, and the website link is nofollow. (estimate: none)
- [ ] **Mohammed + Matt:** Skip Yext. Self-serve plans still list $199 to $999 a year, and doing the four big listings above by hand costs nothing. (estimate: none)
- [ ] **Mohammed + Matt:** Yellowpages. A free tier exists. Last, if ever. (estimate: 15 minutes, whenever there is a spare one)

## Not doing

From SEO-PLAN.md. Nine things this round skips on purpose, one reason each.

1. Doorway pages, one per town per service. No demand in autocomplete, and the pattern Google's scaled-content guidance targets directly.
2. Sea urchin, mussel or abalone foraging pages. The strongest foraging autocomplete in the whole set, and Matt does not do it.
3. A blog. Informational posts are the worst-hit content format in 2026.
4. llms.txt. Google states plainly that a file like this neither helps nor harms.
5. IndexNow. Bing supports it and Google does not, so it buys nothing here.
6. Review or rating markup. Disallowed for a business marking up reviews about itself.
7. More FAQ markup. The rich result it used to earn is gone. The FAQ text stays on the page because people and the Maps "Ask" panel both still read it.
8. Video markup on the hero loops. They are decorative background loops, not a watch page, and do not meet Google's definition.
9. Course markup on the classes. A few-hour class is not the curriculum of lectures and modules the markup requires.
