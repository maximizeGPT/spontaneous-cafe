The one change worth the most. "Foraging excursions", the site's own phrase, returns none. The word is in the page title and description (in the PR), and your refresh of 2026-09-19 put it in the lede and the body. The h1 is the remaining gap.

- "Into the forest" stop: "Mushroom hunting under the redwoods, with greens and berries in the same baskets. Matt puts the edible one and the thing that looks exactly like it side by side, before either goes near a basket."

## 3. Four pricing questions on the private chef page

All four are confirmed autocomplete phrases, every answer is already on the page, and the build turns each `<details>` into FAQ markup on its own. First person, as the other FAQ answers.

- How much does a private chef cost for a day? "I charge $1,000 for twelve hours, plus ingredients at cost. That buys a foraging or market morning and the dinner it turns into, or breakfast and dinner for a full house. Six hours is $500 and three hours is $300, and I use the same tiers whichever service you book."
- How much does a private chef cost for a dinner party? "Most dinner parties I cook run six hours, which is $500. That covers the shopping, a multi-course dinner for up to twelve and the cleanup after it. I bill ingredients at cost on top of that, with the receipts."
- What is the difference between a personal chef and a private chef? "A personal chef usually cooks meals ahead for the week and leaves them in the fridge. A private chef cooks and serves while you are at the table. I do the second: I arrive with the shopping done, cook in your kitchen, carry each course out, and clear up." (This captures "personal chef", the synonym the site never uses and the Google Business Profile category.)
- Do you do the dishes? "Yes. I clean the kitchen before I leave, and leftovers go into your own containers."

Also the "Where" line under Good to know: "Your vacation rental, second home or full-time home anywhere in the greater Mendocino area, from Fort Bragg down the coast to Elk and inland to Anderson Valley. The other address is Chef Matt's own kitchen in Albion, CA. Further out, ask." ("private chef fort bragg ca" is a confirmed phrase; the towns match the catering page's Travel line.)

- "Wedding catering" as an exact pair once in the body. The lede under "How an event comes together" can read "Wedding catering runs the longest version of this; a retreat dinner can run it in two weeks."

(Bare "catering mendocino" is polluted by the Mendocino Farms sandwich chain; "mendocino county catering" is the clean phrase.)
- A "Retreat catering" section between "Service styles" and "Sample event menus". Five confirmed autocomplete phrases (mendocino retreat center, yoga retreat, wellness retreat, writing retreat, meditation retreat), and the venues on this coast outsource their catering. Draft, third person:

  "Retreat centers, camps and rented houses around greater Mendocino book Matt for a whole stay, every meal for the group. The dietary list is built into the first draft of the menus. He has written retreat menus for twenty to eighty guests and run culinary intensives for hospitality teams. A stay can open with a foraging walk for the whole group, and a cooking class can stand in for one of the dinners."

  One line under it for what is included beyond the paragraph: vegan, gluten free and allergy menus designed alongside the rest, and larger events with a crew. (A bullet list repeating the paragraph was tried and cut.) Link: "Tell Matt about your retreat" to the contact form with the catering service preselected. Needs Matt: that he wants retreats sold this way; the twenty to eighty figure is from his resume.

The quote lists courses, service style, cooks and service staff, rentals and travel, so two weddings of the same size land in different places." Needs Matt if a typical range is to be published (the phrase people search is "how much to cater a wedding for 50 people").
