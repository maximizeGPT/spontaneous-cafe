# Responsive search ad copy

Google limits headlines to 30 characters and descriptions to 90. Counts verified by script,
see `validate.py`. No phone numbers appear in any line, because Google Ads policy disallows
phone numbers in ad text. The number reaches the searcher through a call asset instead.

Every claim below traces to a confirmed fact in `CONTEXT.md` or Matt's resume. Nothing here
depends on an open `<!-- CONFIRM -->` tag.

Pin one headline to position 1 per ad group and leave the rest unpinned. Ad Strength is a
completeness checklist, not a target: Optmyzr's study of roughly 20,000 accounts found
"Excellent" ads posted the worst CPA at $28.68 against "Average" at $12.43.
