# Google Ads, The Spontaneous Cafe. 2026-09-19

Agency-side media plan for the $500 Google Ads offer attached to the Business Profile.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `PLAN.md` | The plan. Budget reality, the go/no-go gate, readiness blockers, the calendar, campaign structure, measurement, unit economics, handover lockdown, open questions. |

## The short version

The $500 is a spend match, not a grant. $500 of cash unlocks $500 of credit, so the media
budget is $1,000 and the cash at risk is $500. The credit's clocks split the money into two
flights, which lands them on the two Mendocino demand peaks: Nov 1 to Dec 20 on cash, then
January and February on credit.

Nothing spends until four blockers clear: DNS, conversion tracking, Matt's answers to the
47 open CONFIRM tags, and Business Profile verification.

Source research for this plan lives in `../seo-2026-09-19/R2-keywords-serp.md`.
