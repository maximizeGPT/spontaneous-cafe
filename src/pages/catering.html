How an event comes together

Weddings run the longest version of this; a retreat dinner can run it in two weeks.

  1. First call

    You talk it through

    Date, venue, head count, budget, and what kind of eating you want people doing.

  2. Weeks out

    Tasting or menu call

    Agree on a written menu and service plan. If you’re interested in a tasting, discuss availability and cost with Matt.

  3. The week of

    He sources it

    Sourcing runs the week of the event: a ranch inland, the boats at Noyo, and whatever the woods have. The final guest count and any deadlines are agreed in your booking terms.

  4. The day

    The crew sets up and cooks

    They arrive early with the kitchen packed in the van, cook, serve, clear, and leave the venue the way they found it. Where there is a lawn and a power outlet instead of a kitchen, they prep off site and finish on arrival.

Service styles

Seated multi-course

A relaxed sequence of plated courses, with staffing planned around your guest count, venue and pace of service.

Family style at long tables

Generous platters at the center of the table, made for sharing and conversation. A welcoming format for weddings, reunions and family gatherings.

Grazing and passed bites

Seasonal bites and shared boards that let guests mingle. The selection and service are planned around the space and length of your event.

Good to know

Guest counts
Matt has catered events from 20 to 350 guests.
Venues
Private homes, rentals, barns, ranches, gardens and rented halls. An unfamiliar venue gets a walk-through before the day.
Staff and rentals
Staffing and equipment needs depend on the venue, guest count and service style. Agree on who will arrange any rentals and what is included in the quote.
Retreats
Multi-day retreats receive a quote based on the schedule, guest count and meals required. See cooking classes for the interactive dinner format.
Dietary needs
Vegan, gluten free and allergy plates are designed with the menu on the first draft. Send the list with your final counts.
Travel
Based in greater Mendocino. Send your venue address so Matt can confirm availability and include any travel costs in the quote.

Questions people ask

Do you do tastings?

Ask Matt whether a tasting would suit your event. The format, availability and any cost can be discussed alongside the menu.

What about drinks?

I do not sell or pour alcohol. Hire a licensed bartender if you want it poured. Tell me the menu and I will say what I would drink with it.

What happens if it rains?

It rains here, and the fog arrives whether or not it does. Every outdoor event gets a wet plan: a tent, a barn, or a room we move to, with the cutoff agreed in advance. The menu stays the same either way.
