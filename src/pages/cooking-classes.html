What a class is

What a class covers.

The first is the knife. How to hold it, how to keep it sharp, and how to cut for an hour without your shoulder hurting. Most home cooks are slow because the knife is dull and the board is in the wrong place.

The second is efficiency. Where things go on the counter, what goes in the pan first, and how to have four things finish at the same time. The third is playing. Matt wants you comfortable enough to open the fridge, look at what is there, and start.

The fourth is cooking with what is available. Matt has run The Spontaneous Cafe since 2009. The menu is agreed with the guest in advance and built from what the farms, the market and the forest have that week. The same habit works on a Tuesday with half an onion and some greens.

Matt, on the class that starts in the forest: “It is my favorite one to teach.”

Knife skills and kitchen efficiency

Half a session at a board with Matt watching your grip. Your own knives get sharpened first, then you work through the cuts you actually use at home. By the end you will have broken down a chicken and cut a case of vegetables.

Before you book

Four questions Matt gets every week.

How many people can take a class?

There is no cap. Two is a good class and so is twelve, and the format changes with the number. Small groups get more time on the board with me. Large groups work better as stations, where each pair takes one dish and I move between them.

Do we need any equipment?

A working stove, a sink and some counter space. I bring knives, boards and small equipment when a kitchen is short. If you want to learn on your own knives, leave them out and I will sharpen them at the start.

Can you work around dietary needs?

Yes, and I plan the class around them from the start. Substituting at the end never works as well. Tell me about allergies, vegetarian or vegan eaters and anything else when you book.

Can I give a class as a gift?

Yes. People book these for birthdays and anniversaries, and the date can stay open until the person receiving it picks one. Call or email me and I will send you something you can hand over.
