What a class covers

A class covers knife skills, kitchen setup and cooking from what’s on hand, in a session built around your group. Matt has trained culinary students, mentored interns and led workshops for hospitality teams. He brings that experience to practical skills you can use at home.

  • Knife grip and the cuts you actually use at home
  • Counter setup so several dishes finish at the same time
  • Cooking from what is in the fridge or the market, with no recipe

A class can end as the dinner the group sits down to, and the four services combine into one booking. Build your own day.

Three class formats

Knife skills and kitchen efficiency

Learn a comfortable grip, useful cuts and a more organized approach to prep. Matt tailors the ingredients and techniques to your experience, interests and dietary needs.

Cook what you found

A full class that starts at the farmers market or in the forest and ends at your table. You agree on a menu direction before the day, then learn how to adapt it to the ingredients available.

Interactive dinner party

Questions people ask

How many people can take a class?

The right group size depends on the kitchen and what you want to learn. Small groups allow more individual instruction; larger groups can work in stations. Send your guest count and venue details so I can suggest a format.

Do we need any equipment?

A working stove, a sink and some counter space. Tell me what equipment is available so we can plan for anything that is missing. If you want to learn on your own knives, leave them out for the class.

Can you work around dietary needs?

Yes, and I plan the class around them from the start. Substituting at the end never works as well. Tell me about allergies, vegetarian or vegan eaters and anything else when you book.

Can I give a class as a gift?

Ask me about arranging a class as a gift. We can discuss the recipient’s interests, the class format and possible dates before you book.
