Privacy

A short policy, because this is a small site and I collect almost nothing.

Effective September 2026

What I collect

Whatever you type into the contact form: your name, email, phone number if you give one, the date and guest count, and your message. It comes to me by email. I use it to answer you and to plan your booking, and I keep it in my inbox the way I keep any other email from a guest.

What I don't do

There are no accounts on this site and nothing to log into. No advertising. I do not sell, rent or trade your details, and I do not add you to a mailing list. There is no tracking pixel and no analytics code on these pages.

Other people's servers

The fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for them and Google sees the request. The site is hosted by a company that keeps ordinary server logs, including IP addresses, for security and traffic counts. Neither of those is something I read.
