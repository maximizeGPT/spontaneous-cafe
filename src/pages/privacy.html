Privacy

How inquiries and basic website information are handled.

Effective September 2026

What the form collects

The contact form asks for a name, an email address, a phone number if you give one, a date, a guest count and a message. Formspree, a US company, receives and stores those submissions and passes them to Matt by email. A small notification function on this site may also forward the same details to Matt as a text message, so a new inquiry reaches him quickly. He uses what you send to answer you and to plan the booking.

Hosting and logs

Vercel hosts this site. Its servers keep ordinary request logs, including your IP address and browser user agent, for a limited time, for security and traffic counts.

Analytics and your choice

When analytics is enabled, we ask your permission before loading Google Analytics through Google Tag Manager. If you accept, Google receives information about the pages you visit, your browser and device, referral sources and actions such as a successful inquiry or a click on a phone or email link. Google also receives connection information, including your IP address, when your browser contacts its service. We use these reports to understand which pages help people find and book Matt’s services.

We do not send your name, email address, phone number or inquiry message to Analytics. Advertising personalization and remarketing are not enabled. Analytics cookies are optional: you can reject them and still browse, call or send an inquiry.

If you see an analytics notice, use its accept or reject buttons. You can change your choice through “Cookie preferences” in the footer. We remember your preference in your browser for up to six months. Withdrawing permission removes this site’s Analytics cookies and reloads the page to stop measurement. If no notice or preferences control appears, Analytics is not enabled for this visit.

There are no visitor accounts and nothing to log into. Fonts are served directly from this site. Your inquiry details are not sold or traded, and sending an inquiry does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

Deleting your inquiry

Email chefmattsamuelson@gmail.com and ask for it to be deleted. It comes out of Formspree and out of Matt’s inbox, and you get a note back confirming it.
