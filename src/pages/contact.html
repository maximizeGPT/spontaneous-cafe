Before you write

Three things people ask first.

How far ahead should I book?

A few weeks is comfortable. Summer weekends and the holidays go earliest, and mushroom season fills up once the rain starts. If your dates are close, ask anyway. Matt has taken bookings the same week and cooked dinner the next night.

What happens after I send this?

Matt reads it and writes back, usually within a day or two. He asks what you cannot eat and where you're staying. Then he sends a plan for the day or the meal with a price on it. Nothing is booked until you say yes to that.

Is there a deposit?

For a dated booking, yes. The amount depends on the size of the job, and Matt puts it in writing before you pay anything.
