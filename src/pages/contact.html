Questions people ask

How far ahead should I book?

A few weeks is comfortable. Summer weekends and the holidays go earliest, and mushroom season fills up once the rain starts. Weddings and retreats book months to a year out; the catering page has the detail. If your dates are close, ask anyway. I have taken bookings the same week and cooked dinner the next night.

What happens after I send this?

I read it and write back, usually within a day or two. I will ask what you cannot eat and where you are staying. Then you get a plan for the day or the meal with a price on it. Nothing is booked until you say yes to that.

Is there a deposit?

For a dated booking, yes. The amount depends on the size of the job, and I put it in writing before you pay anything.
