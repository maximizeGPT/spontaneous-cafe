The Spontaneous Cafe · since 2009

A private chef on the Mendocino coast.

Chef Matt Samuelson forages and shops in the morning, cooks in the afternoon, serves the courses, and washes up before he goes.

How a dinner works

Dinner happens at your home or your rental, or at Chef Matt’s home kitchen in Albion, CA. Guests who would rather not host, or whose rental kitchen is short on burners and counter space, come to him instead.

Matt shops the farm stands and the market, forages what the woods have that week, then cooks that afternoon with his own knives and pans. Courses come out one at a time straight to the table, and he clears the kitchen before he leaves.

  • Date, head count, address and dietary needs collected in advance
  • Shopping and foraging done before he arrives
  • Courses cooked, plated and served in your kitchen
  • Kitchen cleaned and leftovers left in your own containers

Who books a private chef

Most nights are for couples and families in vacation rentals. Weekend groups up from San Francisco book the night everyone is finally under one roof, and birthdays and anniversaries move a restaurant reservation into the kitchen instead. A steady share of the work comes back through guests who passed the number on.

A dinner can start with a foraging morning the same day, and the four services combine into one booking.

The menu

How the menu is set

The menu is set in advance, from what is at its best the week you are here. Whole foods, local, organic where he can get it. Dietary needs and allergies go into the plan at the start: vegan, raw, gluten free, allergies. All of it is workable with notice.

Send a few photos of the kitchen and a note about the equipment available. Matt will plan the meal around the space and discuss anything else needed.

Nettle soup, crème fraîche, beach salt

Morels, spring onion, cream on grilled bread

Spring lamb, asparagus, potatoes cooked in the coals

Lamb from a ranch inland. Asparagus from the market in town.

To finish

Strawberries, honey cream

Late spring only, once the coast has warmed up enough to ripen them.

Good to know

Where
Your vacation rental, second home or full-time home in the greater Mendocino area. The other address is Chef Matt’s own kitchen in Albion, CA. Further out, ask.
Group size
One person up to twelve, and solo diners are welcome. Above twelve, inquire with Matt directly. Any additional staffing is discussed as part of your quote.
Kitchen needs
A working stove, a sink, and some counter space. An oven helps but is not required.
Dietary needs
Vegan, raw, gluten free, allergies, and picky eaters. Send the list with the booking.
Timing
Summer and holiday weekends fill first, so allow a couple of weeks. Midweek dates often work on short notice.
What he brings
Knives, pans, whatever the kitchen turns out to be missing, and all the food.

Questions people ask

Do you shop, or do I?

I shop and I forage, choosing ingredients from local farms, markets and what is in season.

How many people will you cook for?

Anything from one person up to twelve. Solo diners are welcome, and plenty of my dinners are for two, which three hours covers as a two-course dinner. If there are more than twelve of you, write to me directly and we will work out what the night needs.

Do you serve the courses, or only cook them?

I plate in the kitchen, carry each course to the table, and clear it. For larger groups, we’ll discuss any additional service help and include it in the quote.

Can you cook across several days?

Yes. I can cook over a weekend or a week, and I plan the menus so nothing repeats.
