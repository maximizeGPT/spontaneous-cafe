How it works

You pick the date. I handle the rest of the day.

The same five steps every time, whether it is dinner for two or a full house.

  1. When you book

    You tell me the date and who is coming

    I need the date, the head count, the address, and anything anyone cannot eat. That is enough to start.

  2. That morning

    I shop and forage

    Farm stands on the back roads, the market in town, and whatever is up in the woods that week. The menu is settled by the time I load the car.

  3. Late afternoon

    I arrive and cook in your kitchen

    I bring my own knives and pans and set up on your counter. Prep runs a few hours before the first course goes out.

  4. Evening

    You sit down

    Courses come out one at a time, plated in the kitchen and carried to the table. Nobody at the table gets up for anything.

  5. After

    I clean up and leave

    Dishes washed, counters wiped, the kitchen the way I found it. Leftovers stay with you in your own containers.

Who books it

Mostly people on a trip, and the people they tell afterwards.

Four kinds of booking come in more often than anything else.

Couples and families in rentals

Most of my dinners happen in vacation rentals on the Mendocino coast. Some are for two people at a small table. Some are for a house with three generations in it and a kitchen island covered in car keys.

Weekends up from San Francisco

People drive up Friday afternoon and want one night nobody has to plan, shop for or clean after. They book the dinner for the night the whole group is finally in the house.

Birthdays and anniversaries

A dinner at home instead of a restaurant reservation forty minutes down the coast. If there is a cake, a favorite dish or a bottle someone has been saving, tell me and I will build the menu around it.

Referrals

A large share of my work comes from guests who ate one dinner and passed my number on. Rental owners and property managers send people too.

The menu

I write it the night before, sometimes the morning of.

Until I have walked the farm stands I do not know what is out on the shelf, and until I have been up on the ridge I do not know whether the chanterelles came. So the menu gets written last, after the shopping. That is where the name of the business came from.

Whole foods, local, organic when I can get it. Dietary needs and allergies go into the plan at the beginning, so tell me when you book. Vegan, gluten free, no dairy, a nine year old who eats nothing green. All of it is workable if I know early.

I have cooked six courses out of a rental kitchen with two working burners and one dull knife. Whatever the kitchen is missing, I bring.

Good to know

The six things people ask before they book.

Where
Vacation rentals, second homes and full-time homes in the greater Mendocino area. Further out, ask me and I will tell you honestly whether the drive works.
Group size
Two people up to a full house. I have no fixed cap. Past about twelve I bring a second pair of hands.
Kitchen needs
A working stove, a sink, and some counter space. An oven helps and is not required. I have cooked good dinners on two burners.
Dietary needs
Vegan, vegetarian, gluten free, dairy free, allergies, and picky kids. Send the list when you book and it shapes the menu from the first draft.
Timing
Summer and holiday weekends fill first, so give me a couple of weeks if you can. Midweek dates often work on short notice.
What I bring
Knives, pans, and whatever the kitchen turns out to be missing. Serving pieces if you need them. All the food.

Questions

Four I get most weeks.

Do you shop, or do I?

I shop and I forage. You never go to a store. Ingredients come back to you at cost with the receipts, on top of the day rate.

Will you cook for only two people?

Yes, and plenty of my dinners are for two. The day rate is the same for two as for ten, because the day is the same length either way.

Do you serve the courses, or only cook them?

I plate in the kitchen, carry each course to the table, and clear it. For a larger group I bring someone to help with service so the food keeps moving.

Can you cook more than dinner in a day?

The rate covers a day of my time. Some houses spend it all on one long dinner. Others have me in for breakfast, then again in the evening.
