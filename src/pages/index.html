An example foraging day

  1. Morning, low tide

    Salt from the beach

    At low tide they harvested sea salt off the rocks to use at dinner that night.

  2. Late morning

    Mushrooms on the ridge

    In the woods, Matt showed them which mushrooms were edible and how to tell two lookalikes apart.

  3. Midday

    Farm stands and the market

    They stopped at farm stands along the back roads, then the farmers market for what the stands didn’t have.

  4. Evening

    Dinner in their rental

    Matt cooked a multi-course farm-to-table meal in their kitchen from everything gathered that day.

The twelve-hour day, $1,000 for the group

Where the food comes from

Matt buys from small local farms and the farmers market, and forages mushrooms, berries, greens and salt from the coast and the forest. The menu is agreed with the guest in advance, built from whatever the farms, the market and the forest have that week.

Rockfish from the harbor. Fennel from the roadside.

Main

Grass-fed lamb, wild greens, potatoes cooked in the coals

Lamb from a ranch inland. Greens gathered on the walk in.

To finish

Huckleberry galette, honey cream

Huckleberries picked the day before. Honey from a neighbor’s hives.
