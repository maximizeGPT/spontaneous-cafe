About Matt

Matt started in Los Angeles and made his way up the California coast, finding his home in Mendocino over 25 years ago. He’s never left. The ocean and forests drew him in; the community made him stay.

A chef, teacher and forager with retreat kitchen experience on four continents, he cooks with what the coast offers: harvests throughout the year, produce from nearby farms, and mushrooms, greens and berries he gathers himself.
