Foraging

An example foraging day

  1. Morning, low tide

    Salt from the beach

    At low tide they harvested sea salt off the rocks to use at the table.

  2. Late morning

    Mushrooms on the ridge

    In the woods, Matt showed them which mushrooms were edible and how to tell two lookalikes apart.

  3. Midday

    Farm stands and the market

    They stopped at farm stands along the back roads, then the farmers market for what the stands didn’t have.

  4. Evening

    Dinner in their rental

    Matt cooked a multi-course farm-to-table meal in their kitchen from everything gathered that day.
