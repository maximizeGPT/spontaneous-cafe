About Matt

Matt started in Los Angeles and made his way up the California coast, finding his home in Mendocino over twenty-five years ago. He’s never left. The ocean and forests drew him in; the community made him stay.

A chef, teacher and forager with retreat kitchen experience on four continents, he cooks with what the coast offers: year-round harvests, produce from nearby farms, and mushrooms, greens and berries he gathers himself.
