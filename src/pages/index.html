A day, start to finish

A couple booked a full day on their engagement trip.

The order below is the order it happened in.

  1. Morning, low tide

    Salt from the beach

    They walked down at low tide and harvested sea salt off the rocks. It went home in a jar and onto the table that night.

  2. Late morning

    Mushrooms on the ridge

    Into the woods with baskets. Matt shows you what is edible, what is not, and how to tell two lookalikes apart in the hand.

  3. Midday

    Farm stands and the market

    Unstaffed farm stands on the back roads, where you leave cash in a box. Then the farmers market for whatever the stands did not have.

  4. Evening

    Dinner in their rental

    Matt cooked a multi-course farm-to-table meal in their kitchen from everything gathered that day. They sat down.

Where the food comes from

Small farms, honor boxes, the forest and the shore.

Matt buys from small local farms, a couple of larger ones, and farm stands so small nobody staffs them. You leave the money in a box. Most mornings there is nobody else there. The rest he harvests: mushrooms and greens from the forest, salt and sea vegetables from the coast, huckleberries in the weeks they are on.

Menus follow what showed up that week. He writes them the night before, sometimes the morning of. Whole foods, local and organic, cooked with whatever equipment a house already has.

About

Matt Samuelson has cooked on the North Coast since 2008.

He has foraged for more than thirty years, in Northern California and a long way outside it, and taught cooking for twenty-three of them, including at Living Light in Fort Bragg and at Esalen. In 2009 he named the work The Spontaneous Cafe, because the menu is decided by what shows up.

One weekend he cooked for the town of Mendocino for free. Most weeks the guests are people who drove up from San Francisco, or friends of theirs who heard about the food.
