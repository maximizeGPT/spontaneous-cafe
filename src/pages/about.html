About Matt

I’m Matt. I forage and I cook.

Retreat kitchens on four continents, a journey up the California coast, and over twenty-five years calling Mendocino home.

Mendocino

I started in Los Angeles and kept moving north along the California coast until I found Mendocino over twenty-five years ago. I’ve never left. The ocean, the forests and the quiet drew me in, but the people made it home.

There’s a generosity to this community that I love. Growers, cooks, artists and neighbors care about what they make and share what they know. Cooking here means knowing the people behind the ingredients, stopping at a farm stand, talking at the market, and gathering around a table with something good to eat.

The mild coastal climate makes it possible to find something to harvest all year round. What’s available changes with the weather and the season: greens, berries, mushrooms, and produce from the farms nearby. After all these years, that still shapes how I cook. I start with what’s here and let it lead the meal.
