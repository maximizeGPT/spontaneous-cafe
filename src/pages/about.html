About Matt

I’m Matt. I forage and I cook.

Retreat kitchens on four continents, then twenty-five years on the California coast, settled in Mendocino since 2009.

Career timeline

  1. 2000 to 2004

    Living Light Culinary Arts Institute, Fort Bragg

    Head chef and culinary instructor. Two or three meals a day for twenty to forty students and guests, and the curriculum behind them.

  2. 2006 to 2007

    Transsiberian, film production

    Personal chef to the cast on location.

  3. 2006 to present

    High Integrity Foods

    Co-founder of an organic cold-pressed chocolate line.

  4. 2008 to 2009

    Living Light, second stint

    Head chef and culinary instructor again in Fort Bragg.

  5. 2009

    Mendocino, and The Spontaneous Cafe

    Settled on this coast, started the business, and studied foraging formally here.

  6. 2011 to 2015

    Alive & Radiant Foods

    Senior R&D chef on raw snacks distributed across the US and Canada. Designed the production kitchen.

  7. 2015 to 2017

    Flow Restaurant and Lounge, Mendocino

    Executive chef. Menu rebuilt around vegan, raw and gluten free dishes, with sourcing partnerships among local farmers.

  8. 2017 to 2019

    Living Light, third stint

    Head chef and culinary instructor, back in the Fort Bragg kitchen.

  9. Throughout

    Retreat catering and consulting

    Retreat menus for twenty to eighty guests and culinary intensives for hospitality teams, in North America, South America, Asia and Australia.

Peru, Thailand, India

I have cooked for a living on four continents. In Peru I was the chef at yoga retreats, three meals a day for a house full of people I had not met, most of the table on some version of no gluten, no dairy or no meat.

I also cooked in Thailand and India, and the retreat work has never stopped.

Los Angeles and a film set

In Los Angeles I cooked privately for several celebrity clients. I am not going to name them on a website.

On the film Transsiberian I was the personal chef, feeding Woody Harrelson and the rest of the cast on location.

Mendocino

I moved north in stages over about twenty-five years. Foraging is what changed when I got here. I had been picking things for years before this coast, and Mendocino is where I studied it formally, the plants, the mushrooms and the shore, with as much time on the reading as on the walking. Both halves matter when you are looking at two mushrooms that are easy to mistake for each other.

The cooking runs off that. I buy from a handful of small farms and the farmers market, meat and fish included. The salt, the mushrooms, the greens and the berries I go and get.

One weekend I cooked for the town of Mendocino for free.

Teaching

I have taught cooking since 2000. At Living Light it was raw and plant-based technique and the curriculum for the program. At Esalen, down the coast in Big Sur, it was cooking for a house full of people and teaching while doing it.
